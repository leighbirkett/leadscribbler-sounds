University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios: Musical Instrument Samples
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html

The strings (violin, viola, cello and double bass, bowed and plucked), woodwinds (flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon), brass
(French horn, trumpet, trombone, tuba), vibraphone and marimba in this collection come from these recordings, most of
them made in the university's anechoic chamber. The university's own terms, from the page above:

  "These recordings ... may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

They have been freely available from the university since 1997. With thanks to the Electronic Music Studios and the
players who recorded them.

LeadScribbler cuts the recordings' chromatic runs into single notes (iowa_split.py), takes out the chamber's low
rumble, brings each note into tune, gives held notes a seamless loop, and saves them small (build_sounds.py).
